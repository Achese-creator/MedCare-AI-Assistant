SECTION 1: GENERAL HOSPITAL QUESTIONS
=========================================================

Q1: What type of hospital is MedCare Specialist Hospital?
------------------------------------------------------
A: MedCare Specialist Hospital is a private multi-specialty
   hospital located in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria.
   We offer a broad range of medical services including
   general medicine, specialist consultations, surgery,
   diagnostics, maternal care, pediatrics, physiotherapy,
   dental care, and 24/7 emergency services. The hospital
   was established in 2014 and has a capacity of 180 beds.


Q2: Where is MedCare Specialist Hospital located?
--------------------------------------------------
A: We are located in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria.
   For specific directions, please call us at +234 800 MEDCARE
   or visit our website at www.medcarespecialist.com.

Q5: What departments does MedCare Specialist Hospital have?
-----------------------------------------------------------
A: We operate twelve (12) departments:

   1.  General Medicine
   2.  Cardiology
   3.  Pediatrics
   4.  Obstetrics & Gynecology
   5.  Orthopedics
   6.  Surgery

   12. Emergency Department

Q6: Does MedCare Specialist Hospital accept walk-in patients?
-------------------------------------------------------------
A: Yes. We accept walk-in patients across all outpatient
   departments during reception hours. However, patients
   with scheduled appointments are given priority. Walk-in
   patients are attended to on a first-come, first-served
   basis and may experience longer wait times during
   peak hours.

   Walk-in hours:
     Monday – Friday:    8:00 AM – 7:00 PM
     Saturday:           9:00 AM – 4:00 PM

   The Emergency Department accepts walk-in patients
   at all times, 24 hours a day.


Q7: Is MedCare Specialist Hospital a government or
    private hospital?
--------------------------------------------------
A: MedCare Specialist Hospital is a privately owned and
   operated multi-specialty hospital. We are not affiliated
   with any government health institution, though we do
   accept patients under the NHIA (National Health Insurance
   Authority) scheme alongside other private HMOs.


Q8: How many beds does the hospital have?
-----------------------------------------
A: MedCare Specialist Hospital has a capacity of 180 beds
   across its various wards including the General Ward,
   Private Rooms, Maternity Ward, Pediatric Ward,
   ICU, and Post-Operative Recovery Ward.


Q9: Does the hospital have an ICU (Intensive Care Unit)?
--------------------------------------------------------
A: Yes. MedCare Specialist Hospital has an Intensive Care
   Unit (ICU) for patients who require close monitoring
   and advanced medical support. The ICU is staffed around
   the clock by trained medical and nursing personnel.
   Access to the ICU is restricted to immediate family
   members only and is subject to the attending doctor's
   authorization.


Q10: What languages are services provided in?
---------------------------------------------
A: Services at MedCare Specialist Hospital are primarily
   provided in English. Staff members may also communicate
   in Pidgin English and other local languages to assist
   patients who are more comfortable in their native tongue.

SECTION 2: APPOINTMENTS
=========================================================

Q11: How do I book an appointment at MedCare Specialist Hospital?
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A: You can book an appointment in three ways:

   1. Online: Visit www.medcarespecialist.com and click
      "Book an Appointment"
   2. By Phone: Call +234 800 MEDCARE during reception hours
   3. Walk-in: Visit the hospital in person during operating
      hours

   For detailed steps, please refer to our Appointment
   Guide document.


Q12: Can I book an appointment for someone else?
------------------------------------------------
A: Yes. You may book an appointment on behalf of a family
   member, dependent, or friend by providing their personal
   details during the booking process. Please ensure you
   have their consent and relevant medical information
   on hand.


Q13: How early should I arrive for my appointment?
--------------------------------------------------
A: We recommend arriving at least 15 minutes before your
   scheduled appointment time. This allows enough time
   for registration, document verification, and any
   pre-consultation preparations.


Q14: What happens if I am more than 20 minutes late for
     my appointment?
-------------------------------------------------------
A: Patients who arrive more than 20 minutes late for their
   scheduled appointment may have their appointment
   rescheduled. You may be seen at the end of the day's
   schedule if time and the doctor's availability permit.
   If you know you will be running late, please call us
   as soon as possible at +234 800 MEDCARE.


Q15: How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?
--------------------------------------------------
A: To cancel or reschedule, please contact us at least
   24 hours before your appointment:

   By Phone:  Call +234 800 MEDCARE during reception hours
   By Email:  Send an email to support@medcarespecialist.com
              with the subject line:
              "Cancellation Request – [Your Name] – [Date]"
              or
              "Reschedule Request – [Your Name] – [Date]"

   Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the
   appointment may attract a partial consultation fee.
   Please refer to our Appointment Guide for full details.


Q16: Is there a fee to book an appointment?
-------------------------------------------
A: Booking itself is free. However, a consultation fee
   is charged when you arrive for your appointment and
   is paid at the cashier's desk before seeing the doctor.
   Consultation fees vary by department and specialist level.
   Please call us for specific fee information.


Q17: Can I choose a specific doctor for my appointment?
------------------------------------------------------
A: Yes. You may request a specific consultant when booking.
   Please note that availability cannot always be guaranteed.
   If your preferred doctor is unavailable, we will suggest
   a suitable alternative consultant.


Q18: Do I need an appointment for laboratory tests?
---------------------------------------------------
A: Most laboratory tests are available on a walk-in basis
   during laboratory operating hours (7:00 AM – 9:00 PM daily).
   However, certain specialized tests may require prior
   scheduling. Please call +234 800 MEDCARE to confirm
   whether your specific test requires an appointment.


Q19: Do I need an appointment for the pharmacy?
-----------------------------------------------
A: No. The pharmacy operates on a walk-in basis daily
   from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Simply walk in with your
   prescription or request OTC medications directly
   from the pharmacy counter.


Q20: Can I book an appointment on Sunday?
-----------------------------------------
A: The reception is closed on Sundays, so phone bookings
   made on Sunday will be processed on Monday morning.
   However, you can book online at any time through
   our website at www.medcarespecialist.com.
   For medical emergencies on Sunday, please call
   112 or +234 800 MEDCARE — our Emergency Department
   is open 24/7.

SECTION 3: BILLING AND PAYMENTS
=========================================================

Q21: What payment methods does MedCare Specialist Hospital accept?
------------------------------------------------------------------
A: We accept the following payment methods:

   - Cash (Nigerian Naira)
   - Debit Card (Visa, Mastercard, Verve)
   - POS Terminal
   - Bank Transfer
   - Health Insurance / HMO (accepted plans only)

   Cryptocurrency and personal cheques are not accepted.


Q22: When do I pay for services?
---------------------------------
A: Payment timing depends on the type of service:

   - Outpatient consultations: Before seeing the doctor
   - Laboratory and radiology: Before tests are processed
   - Pharmacy: At the point of dispensing
   - Inpatient admission: A deposit is required at admission;
     final balance is settled at discharge
   - Emergency: Initial stabilization is provided regardless
     of payment; billing is addressed once stable


Q23: Can I request an itemized invoice?
---------------------------------------
A: Yes. You can request an itemized invoice at the cashier's
   desk, by phone, or by emailing support@medcarespecialist.com.
   Invoices are useful for insurance reimbursement, corporate
   billing, and personal record keeping.


Q24: Are electronic receipts available?
----------------------------------------
A: Yes. Electronic receipts can be sent to your registered
   phone number via SMS or to your email address upon request.
   Please inform the cashier at the time of payment that
   you would like an electronic receipt.


Q25: What is the refund policy?
--------------------------------
A: Refunds are processed within seven (7) working days
   where applicable. Eligible situations include:

   - Overpayment by the patient
   - Cancellation of a pre-paid service by the hospital
   - Duplicate payments
   - Insurance settlement covering a previously self-paid service

   Non-refundable situations include:
   - Consultation fees after the consultation has occurred
   - Lab or radiology fees after tests have been processed
   - Dispensed pharmacy medications

   To request a refund, contact the billing desk in person
   or email support@medcarespecialist.com with your receipt
   and reason for the refund.


Q26: Can I pay my hospital bill in installments?
-------------------------------------------------
A: Installment payments are not standard policy but may
   be considered for inpatient cases on a case-by-case basis.
   Please speak with the billing manager to discuss your
   specific situation.


Q27: What happens if I cannot pay at discharge?
-----------------------------------------------
A: Please inform the billing team as soon as possible.
   They will work with you to find a suitable arrangement.
   However, please note that discharge documents, prescriptions,
   and medical reports may be withheld until the outstanding
   balance is resolved.


Q28: Do you charge extra for using a debit card or POS?
-------------------------------------------------------
A: No. MedCare Specialist Hospital does not apply additional
   charges for debit card or POS payments.

Q29: Which insurance providers does MedCare Specialist
     Hospital accept?

A: We currently accept the following HMOs and insurance

   1. NHIA (National Health Insurance Authority)

   4. Avon HMO

   receive care and pay out of pocket. We will provide

Q30: What do I need to bring to use my insurance?
-------------------------------------------------
A: Please bring the following:

   - Valid HMO or insurance ID card (physical or digital)
   - Your enrollee ID number
   - Referral or authorization letter (if required by
     your insurer)
   - Valid government-issued ID

   Please inform the receptionist that you are an insured
   patient at registration before any services are rendered.


Q31: Do I need pre-authorization for specialist consultations?
-------------------------------------------------------------
A: This depends on your specific insurance plan. Some HMOs
   require pre-authorization or a referral letter before
   covering specialist consultations. Please contact your
   insurance provider before your visit to confirm
   whether pre-authorization is needed.


Q32: What happens if my insurance does not cover a service?
-----------------------------------------------------------
A: Any service not covered by your insurance plan must be
   paid out of pocket using any of our accepted payment
   methods. The billing team will inform you in advance
   if a service falls outside your coverage so you can
   make an informed decision before proceeding.


Q33: What if I forgot my insurance card?
----------------------------------------
A: For non-emergency services, you will generally need your
   insurance card to access HMO-covered care. You may be
   asked to pay out of pocket and seek reimbursement from
   your insurer afterward.

   For emergency services, care will be provided immediately
   regardless of your insurance card. You can present your
   insurance details after you are stable and the billing
   team will assist with claims processing.


Q34: Can my dependents use my insurance plan at MedCare?
--------------------------------------------------------
A: Only enrollees and dependents officially listed under your
   insurance plan are eligible. Please confirm with your
   insurer which family members are covered under your plan
   before visiting.

SECTION 5: VISITING PATIENTS

Q35: What are the visiting hours at MedCare Specialist Hospital?
----------------------------------------------------------------
A: Visiting hours are as follows:

   General Ward:          4:00 PM – 7:00 PM daily
   Maternity Ward:        4:00 PM – 6:00 PM daily
   Pediatric Ward:        4:00 PM – 6:00 PM daily
                          (One guardian allowed 24/7)

   Private Rooms:         10:00 AM – 8:00 PM
                          (Subject to patient's condition)

Q36: How many visitors can a patient receive at one time?
---------------------------------------------------------
A: In the General Ward, a maximum of two (2) visitors are
   allowed per patient at any one time. In the ICU, only
   one (1) visitor is permitted at a time. Visitors must
   take turns if more people wish to see the patient.


Q37: Can children visit patients in the hospital?
-------------------------------------------------
A: Children under 12 years are generally discouraged from
   visiting wards due to infection control concerns.
   Exceptions may be made for immediate family members
   visiting critically ill patients, subject to approval
   by the ward nurse or attending physician.


Q38: Can I stay overnight with a patient?
-----------------------------------------
A: Overnight stays are generally not permitted for visitors
   in the General Ward. However:

   - One parent or guardian may stay overnight with a
     hospitalized child in the Pediatric Ward
   - Spouses of maternity patients may be granted extended
     access — please inquire at the maternity nurses' station
   - For ICU patients, overnight stays may be arranged
     in exceptional compassionate circumstances at the
     discretion of the medical team


Q39: Can I bring food for a patient?
------------------------------------
A: Yes, but only if approved by the patient's nurse or doctor.
   Patients on special diets or nil-by-mouth (NBO) orders
   must not be given outside food without medical clearance.
   Please always check with the nursing team before bringing
   food or drinks for an admitted patient.


Q40: Is smoking allowed on hospital premises?
---------------------------------------------
A: No. MedCare Specialist Hospital is a strictly smoke-free
   environment. Smoking is prohibited on all hospital premises
   including wards, corridors, restrooms, car parks, and
   outdoor areas. Visitors found smoking on the premises
   will be asked to leave immediately.

SECTION 6: LABORATORY SERVICES
=========================================================

Q41: What laboratory tests are available at MedCare?
----------------------------------------------------
A: Our laboratory offers a comprehensive range of tests
   including but not limited to:

   - Full Blood Count (FBC)
   - Lipid Profile
   - Blood Sugar (Fasting and Random)
   - Malaria Test (RDT and Microscopy)
   - Urinalysis
   - Liver Function Test (LFT)
   - Kidney Function Test (KFT)
   - Electrolytes, Urea and Creatinine
   - Thyroid Function Tests
   - HIV Screening
   - Hepatitis B and C Screening
   - Cardiac Biomarkers (Troponin, CK-MB)
   - ECG (Electrocardiogram)
   - HbA1c
   - Pregnancy Tests (urine and blood)
   - Stool Microscopy
   - Culture and Sensitivity tests
   - Hormonal tests (FSH, LH, Prolactin, Testosterone, PSA)
   - Pre-employment and antenatal screening panels

   For the full list of available tests, please refer to
   our Laboratory Services document or call +234 800 MEDCARE.


Q42: Do I need to fast before a blood test?
-------------------------------------------
A: It depends on the test. The following tests require fasting:

   - Fasting Blood Sugar:      Fast for 8–10 hours
   - Lipid Profile:            Fast for 9–12 hours
   - Liver Function Test:      Fast for 8 hours
   - Pre-employment Panel:     Fast for 8–10 hours
   - Uric Acid:                Fast for 4 hours

   Tests that do NOT require fasting include:
   - Full Blood Count, HbA1c, Thyroid Function Tests,
     Malaria Tests, HIV Screening, Urinalysis, and most
     infectious disease screenings

   Water is permitted during all fasting periods.
   Please confirm with the laboratory or your doctor
   before your test.


Q43: How long does it take to get laboratory results?
-----------------------------------------------------
A: Result turnaround times are as follows:

   - Routine tests:        2–4 hours (same day)
   - Culture tests:        24–72 hours
   - Specialized tests:    Next day or as indicated
   - Emergency tests:      Within 1 hour (prioritized)

   Results can be collected in person, received via email,
   or sent directly to your attending doctor.


Q44: What are the laboratory operating hours?
---------------------------------------------
A: The laboratory is open daily from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM,
   seven days a week. Emergency laboratory services are
   available after hours through the Emergency Department.


Q45: Can someone else collect my lab results for me?
----------------------------------------------------
A: Yes. The authorized person must present your original
   receipt and a written authorization letter signed by you.
   Results will not be released to unauthorized individuals.

Q52: What is the emergency contact number?

A: For emergencies, please call:

   112 (National Emergency Number)

Q53: Does MedCare Specialist Hospital have an ambulance?
--------------------------------------------------------
A: Yes. Ambulance services are available. To request an

Q54: What types of emergencies does MedCare handle?

A: Our Emergency Department is equipped to handle:

-------------------------------------------------
