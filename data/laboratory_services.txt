HOW TO ACCESS LABORATORY SERVICES
=========================================================
Patients can access laboratory services in the following ways:

1. DOCTOR'S REQUEST (In-Hospital Patients)
   - Your attending doctor will issue a laboratory request form
     specifying the tests required
   - Take the request form to the laboratory reception desk
   - Pay for the requested tests at the cashier's desk
   - Return to the laboratory with your payment receipt
   - Provide your specimen as directed by the laboratory staff

2. WALK-IN TESTING (Self-Request)
   - Patients may request certain routine tests without a
     doctor's referral (e.g., blood sugar, malaria test,
     pregnancy test, urinalysis)
   - Walk in during operating hours and speak with the
     laboratory receptionist
   - Pay at the cashier's desk and proceed with specimen
     collection

3. CORPORATE AND GROUP TESTING
   - Organizations may book group laboratory tests as part
     of pre-employment screening or corporate health packages
   - Contact: support@medcarespecialist.com or +234 800 MEDCARE
   - Group results may be compiled into a consolidated report

AVAILABLE LABORATORY TESTS
=========================================================
The following tests are available at MedCare Specialist Hospital
Laboratory. Tests are grouped by category for easy reference.


---------------------------------------------------------
CATEGORY 1: HEMATOLOGY (BLOOD TESTS)
---------------------------------------------------------
These tests examine the cellular components of blood.

1. Full Blood Count (FBC) / Complete Blood Count (CBC)
   Description:  Measures red blood cells, white blood cells,
                 hemoglobin, hematocrit, and platelets
   Purpose:      Detects anemia, infections, clotting disorders,
                 and blood diseases
   Preparation:  No special preparation required
   Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

2. Blood Group and Genotype
   Description:  Determines ABO blood group, Rhesus factor,
                 and hemoglobin genotype (AA, AS, SS, AC, SC)
   Purpose:      Pre-transfusion screening, pre-marital testing,
                 sickle cell screening
   Preparation:  No special preparation required
   Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

3. Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (ESR)
   Description:  Measures the rate at which red blood cells
                 settle in a test tube
   Purpose:      Detects inflammation, infection, or autoimmune
                 conditions
   Preparation:  No special preparation required
   Turnaround:   Same day (1–2 hours)

4. Prothrombin Time (PT/INR)
   Description:  Measures how long blood takes to clot
   Purpose:      Monitors patients on blood thinners (warfarin),
                 pre-operative assessment, liver disease
   Preparation:  Inform the lab if you are on anticoagulant
                 medication
   Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

5. Peripheral Blood Film (PBF)
   Description:  Microscopic examination of blood cells
   Purpose:      Detects malaria parasites, abnormal blood cells,
                 and blood disorders
   Preparation:  No special preparation required
   Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)


---------------------------------------------------------
CATEGORY 2: CLINICAL CHEMISTRY (METABOLIC TESTS)
---------------------------------------------------------
These tests analyze chemical substances in blood and urine.

6. Blood Sugar (Glucose) — Fasting and Random
   Description:  Measures glucose levels in the blood
   Purpose:      Diagnosis and monitoring of diabetes mellitus
   Preparation:
     - Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS): Fast for 8–10 hours before
       the test (water is allowed)
     - Random Blood Sugar (RBS): No fasting required
   Turnaround:   Same day (1–2 hours)

7. HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)
   Description:  Measures average blood sugar levels over
                 the past 2–3 months
   Purpose:      Long-term diabetes management and diagnosis
   Preparation:  No fasting required
   Turnaround:   Same day to next day

8. Lipid Profile
   Description:  Measures total cholesterol, LDL, HDL,
                 and triglycerides
   Purpose:      Cardiovascular risk assessment and monitoring
   Preparation:  Fast for 9–12 hours before the test
                 (water is allowed)
   Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

9. Liver Function Test (LFT)
   Description:  Measures liver enzymes, bilirubin, albumin,
                 and total protein
   Purpose:      Assesses liver health and detects liver disease
                 or damage
   Preparation:  Fast for 8 hours before the test (water allowed)
   Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

10. Kidney Function Test (KFT) / Renal Function Test (RFT)
    Description:  Measures creatinine, urea, uric acid, and
                  estimated GFR (glomerular filtration rate)
    Purpose:      Assesses kidney health, detects chronic kidney
                  disease, and monitors renal function
    Preparation:  Avoid excessive protein intake 24 hours before
                  the test; stay well hydrated
    Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

11. Electrolytes, Urea and Creatinine (E/U/Cr)
    Description:  Measures sodium, potassium, chloride, bicarbonate,
                  urea, and creatinine levels
    Purpose:      Evaluates fluid balance, kidney function, and
                  electrolyte disorders
    Preparation:  No special fasting required
    Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

12. Thyroid Function Tests (TFT)
    Description:  Measures TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone),
                  T3 (Triiodothyronine), and T4 (Thyroxine)
    Purpose:      Diagnoses hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism,
                  and thyroid disorders
    Preparation:  Take the test in the morning if possible;
                  inform the lab of any thyroid medications
    Turnaround:   Same day to next day

13. Uric Acid
    Description:  Measures uric acid levels in the blood
    Purpose:      Diagnoses and monitors gout and kidney stones
    Preparation:  Fast for 4 hours before the test
    Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

14. C-Reactive Protein (CRP)
    Description:  Measures CRP, a protein produced in response
                  to inflammation
    Purpose:      Detects infections, inflammatory conditions,
                  and monitors treatment response
    Preparation:  No special preparation required
    Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)


---------------------------------------------------------
CATEGORY 3: CARDIAC MARKERS
---------------------------------------------------------
These tests help diagnose and monitor heart conditions.

15. Cardiac Biomarkers (Troponin I / Troponin T)
    Description:  Measures proteins released when heart muscle
                  is damaged
    Purpose:      Diagnosis of heart attack (myocardial infarction)
                  and acute coronary syndrome
    Preparation:  No special preparation required
    Turnaround:   1–2 hours (urgent processing available)

16. Creatine Kinase (CK) and CK-MB
    Description:  Measures enzymes released from damaged muscle,
                  particularly the heart
    Purpose:      Diagnosis and monitoring of heart attack and
                  muscle disorders
    Preparation:  No special preparation required
    Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

17. ECG (Electrocardiogram)
    Description:  Records the electrical activity of the heart
    Purpose:      Detects arrhythmias, heart attacks, and other
                  cardiac conditions
    Preparation:  Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing;
                  avoid applying lotions to the chest area
                  on the day of the test
    Turnaround:   Results available immediately
    Note:         ECG is performed by the laboratory/cardiology
                  team and interpreted by the cardiologist


---------------------------------------------------------
CATEGORY 4: INFECTIOUS DISEASE SCREENING
---------------------------------------------------------
These tests detect bacterial, viral, and parasitic infections.

18. Malaria Test
    a. Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT):
       - Quick screening for malaria antigens
       - Turnaround: 15–30 minutes
    b. Malaria Microscopy (Blood Film):
       - Microscopic examination to identify malaria parasites
         and species
       - Turnaround: 1–2 hours
    Preparation:  No special preparation required

19. HIV Screening (HIV 1 & 2)
    Description:  Detects HIV antibodies/antigens in the blood
    Purpose:      Screening, diagnosis, and pre-operative testing
    Preparation:  No special preparation required
    Turnaround:   Same day (1–2 hours)
    Note:         All HIV tests are conducted with strict
                  confidentiality

20. Hepatitis B Surface Antigen (HBsAg)
    Description:  Detects the presence of Hepatitis B virus
    Purpose:      Screening and diagnosis of Hepatitis B infection
    Preparation:  No special preparation required
    Turnaround:   Same day (1–2 hours)

21. Hepatitis C Antibody (Anti-HCV)
    Description:  Detects antibodies to the Hepatitis C virus
    Purpose:      Screening and diagnosis of Hepatitis C infection
    Preparation:  No special preparation required
    Turnaround:   Same day (1–2 hours)

22. Widal Test (Typhoid Fever Screening)
    Description:  Detects antibodies against Salmonella typhi
    Purpose:      Screening for typhoid fever
    Preparation:  No special preparation required
    Turnaround:   Same day (2–3 hours)

23. H. pylori Test
    Description:  Detects Helicobacter pylori infection
    Purpose:      Diagnosis of stomach ulcers and gastritis
                  caused by H. pylori
    Preparation:  No special preparation required
    Turnaround:   Same day (1–2 hours)

24. VDRL / RPR (Syphilis Screening)
    Description:  Detects antibodies associated with syphilis
    Purpose:      Screening for syphilis (sexually transmitted
                  infection)
    Preparation:  No special preparation required
    Turnaround:   Same day (2–3 hours)


---------------------------------------------------------
CATEGORY 5: URINALYSIS AND STOOL TESTS
---------------------------------------------------------

25. Urinalysis (Urine Full Report)
    Description:  Physical, chemical, and microscopic examination
                  of urine
    Purpose:      Detects urinary tract infections (UTIs), kidney
                  disease, diabetes, and other conditions
    Preparation:
      - Collect a midstream urine sample in a clean, sterile
        container provided by the laboratory
      - First morning urine sample is preferred for best results
      - Do not collect during menstruation if possible
    Turnaround:   Same day (1–2 hours)

26. Urine Culture and Sensitivity
    Description:  Identifies bacteria in urine and tests
                  their response to antibiotics
    Purpose:      Guides antibiotic treatment for UTIs
    Preparation:  Midstream urine sample required; do not
                  begin antibiotics before collecting the sample
    Turnaround:   24–72 hours (culture growth required)

27. Pregnancy Test (Urine)
    Description:  Detects human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG)
                  in urine
    Purpose:      Confirmation of pregnancy
    Preparation:  First morning urine preferred for accuracy
    Turnaround:   15–30 minutes

28. Pregnancy Test (Blood — Beta hCG)
    Description:  Quantitative measurement of hCG in blood
    Purpose:      Confirmation and monitoring of early pregnancy
    Preparation:  No special preparation required
    Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

29. Stool Microscopy (Ova and Parasites)
    Description:  Microscopic examination of stool for parasites,
                  bacteria, and blood
    Purpose:      Detects intestinal infections, parasitic
                  infestations, and gastrointestinal disorders
    Preparation:
      - Collect a fresh stool sample in a clean, dry container
        provided by the laboratory
      - Do not mix with urine or water
      - Deliver sample to the lab within 2 hours of collection
    Turnaround:   Same day (2–4 hours)

30. Stool Culture
    Description:  Identifies bacteria causing gastrointestinal
                  infections
    Purpose:      Guides antibiotic treatment for GI infections
    Preparation:  Fresh stool sample required (as above)
    Turnaround:   24–72 hours


---------------------------------------------------------
CATEGORY 6: HORMONAL AND REPRODUCTIVE TESTS
---------------------------------------------------------

31. Follicle Stimulating Hormone (FSH)
    Description:  Measures FSH levels in the blood
    Purpose:      Assesses reproductive health and fertility
    Preparation:  Test is usually done on day 2–3 of the
                  menstrual cycle (for females); inform the
                  lab of your cycle day
    Turnaround:   Same day to next day

32. Luteinizing Hormone (LH)
    Description:  Measures LH levels in the blood
    Purpose:      Assesses ovulation, fertility, and pituitary
                  function
    Preparation:  As advised by your doctor
    Turnaround:   Same day to next day

33. Prolactin
    Description:  Measures prolactin hormone levels
    Purpose:      Evaluates lactation issues, menstrual disorders,
                  and pituitary function
    Preparation:  Test should be done in the morning; avoid
                  stress and physical activity before the test
    Turnaround:   Same day to next day

34. Testosterone
    Description:  Measures testosterone levels in the blood
    Purpose:      Evaluates male reproductive health, libido,
                  muscle disorders, and hormonal balance
    Preparation:  Test should be done in the morning
    Turnaround:   Same day to next day

35. Prostate Specific Antigen (PSA)
    Description:  Measures PSA levels in the blood
    Purpose:      Prostate cancer screening and monitoring
    Preparation:  Avoid ejaculation and vigorous exercise for
                  48 hours before the test; inform the lab of
                  any prostate procedures or medications
    Turnaround:   Same day to next day

SPECIMEN COLLECTION GUIDELINES
=========================================================
To ensure accurate test results, please follow these general
specimen collection guidelines:

BLOOD SAMPLES:
  - Blood is drawn by a trained phlebotomist at the laboratory
  - Most blood tests require a small sample drawn from a vein
    in the arm
  - Inform the laboratory staff if you:
    * Have a fear of needles or tend to faint during blood draws
    * Are taking any medications (especially blood thinners)
    * Have a bleeding disorder
    * Have difficult veins
  - After the blood draw, apply gentle pressure to the site
    and avoid heavy lifting for a few hours

URINE SAMPLES:
  - Sterile urine collection containers are provided free
    of charge at the laboratory reception
  - A midstream sample is preferred for most urine tests
    (let the first flow of urine pass, then collect the middle
    portion into the container)
  - Label the container with your full name and date of collection
  - Submit the sample to the laboratory within 30 minutes of
    collection or as directed

STOOL SAMPLES:
  - Clean stool collection containers are provided at the
    laboratory reception
  - Collect a fresh sample and deliver it to the laboratory
    within 2 hours
  - Do not allow the sample to mix with urine or water
  - Label the container with your full name and collection date

RESULT COLLECTION
=========================================================
TEST RESULT TURNAROUND:
  - Routine results:        2–4 hours (same day for most tests)
  - Culture tests:          24–72 hours
  - Specialized tests:      Next day or as indicated
  - Emergency tests:        Within 1 hour (prioritized processing)

HOW TO COLLECT YOUR RESULTS:
  1. In-person: Visit the laboratory with your receipt or
     patient ID during operating hours
  2. Via email: Request electronic delivery when submitting
     your sample — results will be sent to your registered
     email address
  3. Via doctor: Results for in-hospital patients are sent
     directly to the requesting doctor

IMPORTANT:
  - Test results will only be released to:
    * The patient (with valid ID)
    * An authorized person (with patient's written consent)
    * The requesting doctor (for in-hospital patients)
  - Results are kept confidential and are not disclosed to
    unauthorized individuals
  - The laboratory does not interpret results over the phone.
    Please consult your doctor for result interpretation

FREQUENTLY ASKED LABORATORY QUESTIONS
=========================================================

Q: Do I need a doctor's referral to get a lab test?
A: Most routine tests can be done without a referral. However,
   specialized or complex tests are best requested through
   your attending doctor.

Q: How do I prepare for a fasting blood test?
A: Do not eat or drink anything except water for the required
   fasting period before your test. Take your test early in
   the morning for convenience.

Q: Can I take my medications before a lab test?
A: This depends on the test. For most tests, you may take
   your regular medications. However, for certain tests
   (e.g., thyroid function, hormone tests), you may be
   advised to take them after the test. Please confirm
   with your doctor or the laboratory staff.

Q: How long does it take to get my results?
A: Most routine results are available the same day within
   2–4 hours. Culture tests take 24–72 hours. Emergency
   tests are processed within 1 hour.

Q: Can someone else collect my results on my behalf?
A: Yes, but they must present your original receipt and a
   written authorization letter signed by you.

Q: Are my test results confidential?
A: Absolutely. All test results are strictly confidential
   and will only be released to the patient or authorized
   individuals.

Q: What if I feel faint during a blood draw?
A: Inform the laboratory staff before the procedure. They
   are trained to assist patients who feel anxious or faint
   and will ensure your comfort and safety throughout.

Q: Can I walk in for a lab test without an appointment?
A: Yes. Most laboratory tests are available on a walk-in
   basis during operating hours (7:00 AM – 9:00 PM daily).
