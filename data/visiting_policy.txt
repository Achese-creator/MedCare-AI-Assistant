MEDCARE SPECIALIST HOSPITAL — VISITING POLICY
=============================================

MedCare Specialist Hospital welcomes visitors and understands the important
role that family and friends play in a patient's recovery. However, to
maintain a safe, calm, and healing environment for all patients, we have
established clear visiting guidelines that all visitors are expected to
follow.

We appreciate your cooperation and understanding.


=========================================================
GENERAL VISITING HOURS
=========================================================

Visiting hours are structured to allow patients adequate rest while
still enabling them to receive support from their loved ones.

General Ward:
  Daily Visiting Hours:   4:00 PM – 7:00 PM

Maternity Ward:
  Daily Visiting Hours:   4:00 PM – 6:00 PM
  (Spouses/partners may be granted extended access — please inquire
  at the nurses' station)

Pediatric Ward:
  One (1) parent or legal guardian is permitted to stay with the child
  at all times
  General visitors:       4:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Intensive Care Unit (ICU):
  Visitors:               Immediate family members only
  Hours:                  As advised by the attending physician
  Number of visitors:     Maximum of two (2) at a time
  Duration per visit:     Maximum 15–20 minutes per visit

Private Rooms:
  Patients in private rooms may receive visitors with more flexibility,
  subject to the patient's condition and doctor's approval
  Recommended hours:      10:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Post-Operative Recovery Ward:
  Visiting is restricted for the first 24 hours post-surgery unless
  authorized by the attending surgeon
  After 24 hours:         Follow General Ward visiting hours

Emergency Department:
  Only one (1) accompanying person is permitted with the patient
  at any given time
  Additional family members may wait in the designated waiting area


=========================================================
VISITOR RULES AND CONDUCT
=========================================================

All visitors are expected to observe the following rules at all times
while on the hospital premises:

1. CHECK IN AT RECEPTION
   - All visitors must check in at the hospital reception or nurses'
     station before proceeding to patient wards
   - Visitors may be required to present a valid ID
   - A visitor's pass or badge may be issued and must be worn visibly
     throughout the visit

2. NUMBER OF VISITORS
   - A maximum of two (2) visitors are allowed per patient at any
     one time in the General Ward
   - Only one (1) visitor at a time is allowed in the ICU
   - Visitors must take turns if more people wish to visit

3. CHILDREN AS VISITORS
   - Children under the age of 12 are generally discouraged from
     visiting wards due to infection control concerns
   - Exceptions may be made for immediate family members visiting
     critically ill patients, subject to approval by the ward nurse
     or attending physician
   - Children allowed in the hospital must be supervised by an adult
     at all times

4. NOISE AND CONDUCT
   - Visitors must maintain a quiet and respectful atmosphere in all
     ward areas
   - Mobile phones should be set to silent mode inside wards
   - Loud conversations, arguments, or disruptive behavior will not
     be tolerated and may result in removal from the premises
   - Please knock before entering a patient's room

5. FOOD AND DRINKS
   - Visitors are welcome to bring food and drinks for patients
     ONLY if approved by the patient's nurse or doctor
   - Patients on special diets or nil-by-mouth (NBO) orders must
     not be given outside food without medical clearance
   - Eating and drinking in ward corridors is not permitted
   - Please use designated cafeteria or waiting areas for meals

6. PERSONAL HYGIENE AND INFECTION CONTROL
   - All visitors must wash or sanitize their hands before entering
     and after leaving patient areas
   - Hand sanitizer dispensers are available at all ward entrances
   - Visitors who are feeling unwell, have a fever, cough, cold,
     or any contagious illness are strongly advised NOT to visit
   - Visitors may be required to wear a face mask in certain wards
     (masks are available at the nurses' station if needed)

7. FLOWERS, GIFTS, AND PERSONAL ITEMS
   - Fresh flowers are not permitted in the ICU or any ward with
     immunocompromised patients
   - Gifts such as books, cards, and packaged snacks are generally
     acceptable
   - Large items of furniture or electrical appliances must be
     approved by hospital management before being brought in
   - The hospital is not responsible for loss or damage of personal
     items brought in by visitors or patients

8. SMOKING AND ALCOHOL
   - MedCare Specialist Hospital is a strictly smoke-free environment
   - Smoking is prohibited on all hospital premises including car parks,
     corridors, restrooms, and outdoor areas
   - Alcohol and intoxicating substances are strictly prohibited
   - Visitors found violating this policy will be asked to leave the
     premises immediately

9. PHOTOGRAPHY AND RECORDING
   - Photography, video recording, or audio recording of patients,
     staff, or hospital facilities without prior written consent is
     strictly prohibited
   - This policy is in place to protect patient privacy and dignity
     in accordance with healthcare confidentiality standards

10. PARKING AND PREMISES
    - Visitors must park only in designated visitor parking areas
    - Obstruction of emergency access routes is strictly prohibited
    - Littering on hospital premises is not allowed
    - Visitors must follow all directional signs and instructions
      from hospital security personnel


=========================================================
SPECIAL VISITING CIRCUMSTANCES
=========================================================

END-OF-LIFE AND CRITICAL CONDITION VISITS
------------------------------------------
- When a patient is in a critical or end-of-life condition, the hospital
  may grant extended visiting access to immediate family members
- This is arranged directly with the ward nurse or attending physician
- A quiet family waiting room is available for families in such situations
- Our patient support team is available to provide guidance and comfort

VISITING PATIENTS IN ISOLATION
--------------------------------
- Patients with infectious or contagious conditions may be placed in
  isolation rooms
- Visiting isolated patients may be restricted or require special
  protective equipment (PPE)
- The ward nurse will advise visitors on the appropriate precautions
  to take before entering an isolation room

VISITING PATIENTS AFTER SURGERY
---------------------------------
- Postoperative patients require extra rest and minimal disturbance
- Visiting is restricted during the first 24 hours following major surgery
- After the initial recovery period, standard visiting hours apply
- Please confirm visiting arrangements with the surgical ward nurse

OUT-OF-HOURS VISITS
--------------------
- Visits outside of standard visiting hours are generally not permitted
- Exceptions may be granted in compassionate circumstances (e.g.,
  critically ill patients) at the discretion of the on-duty nurse
  or doctor
- To request an out-of-hours visit, please speak directly with the
  ward nurse in charge


=========================================================
VISITING THE MATERNITY WARD
=========================================================
- Expectant and new mothers in the maternity ward are entitled to
  privacy and rest
- Spouses and partners are given priority visiting access
- General visitors should be limited to close family members only
- Newborns must not be handled by visitors without the mother's
  and nurse's permission
- Visitors showing any signs of illness must not visit the maternity
  ward to protect newborns and mothers
- Photography of newborns is at the sole discretion of the parents


=========================================================
VISITING THE PEDIATRIC WARD
=========================================================
- One (1) parent or legal guardian is allowed to stay with a
  hospitalized child at all times, including overnight
- Additional visitors must observe standard General Ward visiting hours
  (4:00 PM – 6:00 PM)
- Toys, books, and comfort items from home are allowed but must be
  clean and appropriate
- All adults visiting the pediatric ward must check in at the
  nurses' station and may be asked to show ID


=========================================================
PATIENT PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
=========================================================
- All patients at MedCare Specialist Hospital have the right to privacy
- Information about a patient's medical condition will only be shared
  with the patient and their designated next of kin or authorized person
- Visitors who make inquiries about a patient's condition will be
  directed to speak with the patient directly or their designated contact
- Staff are bound by confidentiality obligations and will not disclose
  patient details to unauthorized individuals


=========================================================
SECURITY
=========================================================
- Hospital security personnel are present on the premises at all times
- All visitors may be subject to security checks at entry points
- Suspicious behavior, unauthorized access to restricted areas, or
  failure to comply with hospital rules may result in removal from
  the premises and, where applicable, involvement of law enforcement
- Restricted areas include: operating theatres, ICU (without authorization),
  staff-only zones, pharmacy storage, and laboratory areas
