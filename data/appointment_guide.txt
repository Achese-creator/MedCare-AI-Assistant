MEDCARE SPECIALIST HOSPITAL — APPOINTMENT GUIDE
================================================

This guide provides everything patients need to know about booking,
managing, rescheduling, and cancelling appointments at MedCare Specialist
Hospital. Please read carefully to ensure a smooth and stress-free
experience.


=========================================================
OVERVIEW
=========================================================
MedCare Specialist Hospital offers flexible appointment options to
accommodate patients with different schedules and needs. Whether you
prefer to book online, call our front desk, or simply walk in, we are
committed to making healthcare access as convenient as possible.

Appointments are available for:
- General Medicine consultations
- Specialist consultations (Cardiology, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, Orthopedics,
  Dental, Physiotherapy, Surgery)
- Health screening and medical checkups
- Corporate health package assessments
- Laboratory tests (where prior booking is required)
- Antenatal care visits
- Follow-up consultations
- Immunization sessions


=========================================================
BOOKING AN APPOINTMENT
=========================================================

OPTION 1: BOOK ONLINE
---------------------
Patients can book appointments through our official website at:
www.medcarespecialist.com

Steps to book online:
1. Visit www.medcarespecialist.com
2. Click on "Book an Appointment" on the homepage
3. Select the department or specialist you wish to see
4. Choose a preferred date and available time slot
5. Fill in your personal details (name, phone number, email address)
6. Confirm your appointment
7. You will receive a confirmation message via SMS and/or email

Note:
- Online booking is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- Confirmation is sent within a few minutes of booking
- Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time


OPTION 2: BOOK BY PHONE
------------------------
Patients can call our reception desk to book an appointment over the phone.

Phone:  +234 800 MEDCARE
Hours:  Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM
        Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
        Sunday: Reception is closed
                (Emergency line remains available: 112)

Steps to book by phone:
1. Call +234 800 MEDCARE during reception hours
2. Inform the receptionist that you would like to book an appointment
3. Provide your full name, phone number, and reason for visit
4. Select your preferred department or doctor
5. Choose a date and time from available slots
6. The receptionist will confirm your appointment verbally and via SMS

Note:
- Please have your insurance card ready if you are an HMO/insurance enrollee
- Phone bookings made on Sunday will be processed on Monday morning


OPTION 3: WALK-IN
-----------------
MedCare Specialist Hospital accepts walk-in patients across all outpatient
departments, subject to doctor availability.

Walk-in hours:
  Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM – 7:00 PM
  Saturday:      9:00 AM – 4:00 PM

Important information for walk-in patients:
- Walk-in patients are attended to on a first-come, first-served basis
- Scheduled appointment patients are given priority
- Wait times for walk-in patients may be longer during peak hours
- You may be asked to return on another day if the department is fully
  booked for the day
- Walk-in is always available for the Emergency Department regardless
  of hours


=========================================================
WHAT TO BRING TO YOUR APPOINTMENT
=========================================================
To ensure a smooth registration process, please come prepared with the
following:

For all patients:
- Valid government-issued ID (National ID card, Voter's card, Driver's
  license, International passport)
- Your appointment confirmation (SMS, email printout, or booking reference
  number)
- Previous medical records or test results (if any)
- A list of current medications you are taking (if any)

For insured/HMO patients:
- Valid HMO or insurance card
- Letter of authorization or referral letter (if required by your insurer)
- Your HMO enrollee ID number

For returning patients:
- Your hospital file number or patient ID (if available)


=========================================================
APPOINTMENT CONFIRMATION
=========================================================
All booked appointments will be confirmed through the following channels:

- SMS to the phone number provided at booking
- Email confirmation (if email was provided)
- You may also call +234 800 MEDCARE to verify your appointment status

If you do not receive a confirmation within 30 minutes of booking online,
please contact us at support@medcarespecialist.com or call +234 800 MEDCARE.


=========================================================
RESCHEDULING AN APPOINTMENT
=========================================================
If you are unable to attend your scheduled appointment and would like to
change the date or time, you may reschedule under the following conditions:

Rescheduling Policy:
- Appointment rescheduling must be done at least 24 hours before the
  scheduled appointment time
- Rescheduling requests made less than 24 hours before the appointment
  may be subject to availability and cannot be guaranteed
- You may reschedule by phone or email (online rescheduling may be
  available via the website)

How to reschedule:
1. Call +234 800 MEDCARE during reception hours, OR
2. Email support@medcarespecialist.com with the subject line:
   "Reschedule Request – [Your Full Name] – [Original Appointment Date]"
3. Provide your booking reference number, preferred new date, and
   preferred new time
4. You will receive a new confirmation once the reschedule is complete

Note:
- Rescheduling is subject to doctor and slot availability
- Patients may reschedule a maximum of two (2) times for the same
  consultation before a new booking fee may apply


=========================================================
CANCELLING AN APPOINTMENT
=========================================================
We understand that plans change. If you need to cancel your appointment,
please notify us as early as possible so that your slot can be offered
to another patient.

Cancellation Policy:
- Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before the appointment
  time to avoid any cancellation charges
- Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment may
  result in a partial consultation fee being charged
- No-shows (failure to attend without prior notice) may be recorded
  on the patient's file

How to cancel:
1. Call +234 800 MEDCARE during reception hours, OR
2. Email support@medcarespecialist.com with the subject line:
   "Cancellation Request – [Your Full Name] – [Appointment Date]"
3. Provide your full name, booking reference number, and reason for
   cancellation (optional)
4. You will receive a cancellation confirmation via SMS or email


=========================================================
LATE ARRIVAL POLICY
=========================================================
MedCare Specialist Hospital respects the time of all patients and
medical staff.

- Patients who arrive more than 20 minutes late for their scheduled
  appointment may have their appointment rescheduled
- A late-arriving patient may be seen at the end of the day's schedule
  if time permits and the doctor is available
- Please call ahead if you know you will be running late:
  +234 800 MEDCARE
- Repeated late arrivals may result in priority scheduling adjustments


=========================================================
EMERGENCY VISITS
=========================================================
Appointments are NOT required for emergency medical situations.

If you or someone around you is experiencing a medical emergency, please:
- Proceed immediately to the Emergency Department (open 24/7)
- Call our emergency line: 112 or +234 800 MEDCARE
- Our emergency team will attend to you immediately upon arrival

Emergency Department entrance is located at the side of the main building
and is clearly signposted.


=========================================================
SPECIAL APPOINTMENT TYPES
=========================================================

ANTENATAL CARE (ANC) APPOINTMENTS
-----------------------------------
- First ANC visit should be booked as early as possible in pregnancy
  (ideally within the first trimester)
- Follow-up ANC visits are scheduled by the OB/GYN doctor
- Patients should bring all previous scan reports and test results
- ANC bookings can be made by phone or online

CORPORATE HEALTH APPOINTMENTS
------------------------------
- Companies interested in staff health screening can contact us directly
- Group appointments and on-site health checks can be arranged
- Contact: support@medcarespecialist.com or +234 800 MEDCARE

PEDIATRIC APPOINTMENTS
------------------------
- A parent or legal guardian must accompany all patients under 18 years
- Please bring the child's immunization card where applicable


=========================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED APPOINTMENT QUESTIONS
=========================================================

Q: Can I book an appointment for someone else?
A: Yes. You can book on behalf of a family member or dependent by
   providing their details during the booking process.

Q: Do I need an appointment for laboratory tests?
A: Most lab tests are walk-in friendly. However, certain specialized
   tests may require prior scheduling. Please call us to confirm.

Q: How early should I arrive for my appointment?
A: We recommend arriving at least 15 minutes before your scheduled time
   to allow time for registration.

Q: Can I choose a specific doctor?
A: Yes. You may request a specific consultant when booking. Availability
   cannot always be guaranteed, and an alternative may be suggested.

Q: What happens if my doctor is unavailable on my appointment day?
A: You will be notified as early as possible and offered an alternative
   appointment time or a substitute consultant.

Q: Is there a booking fee?
A: A consultation fee applies and is paid at registration. Fees vary by
   department and specialist. Please contact us for specific fee information.
