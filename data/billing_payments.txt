MEDCARE SPECIALIST HOSPITAL — BILLING & PAYMENTS
=================================================

MedCare Specialist Hospital is committed to transparent, fair, and
flexible billing practices. We want every patient to fully understand
their financial obligations and available payment options before, during,
and after receiving care.

This document outlines our billing procedures, accepted payment methods,
invoice processes, refund policy, and financial support options.


=========================================================
GENERAL BILLING OVERVIEW
=========================================================
All patients receiving services at MedCare Specialist Hospital will be
billed for the services they receive. Billing may occur at different
points depending on the nature of care:

- Outpatients:    Payment is typically made before or immediately
                  after consultation and services are rendered
- Inpatients:     A deposit may be required upon admission. Final
                  billing is settled at discharge
- Emergency:      Initial stabilization is provided regardless of
                  payment status. Billing is addressed once the
                  patient is stable
- Laboratory &
  Radiology:      Payment is made before tests are processed,
                  unless the patient is covered by an accepted
                  insurance plan
- Pharmacy:       Payment is made at the point of dispensing


=========================================================
ACCEPTED PAYMENT METHODS
=========================================================
MedCare Specialist Hospital accepts the following payment methods
for all services:

1. CASH
   - Nigerian Naira (NGN) is accepted at all billing points
   - Cash payments are made at the cashier's desk located at
     the ground floor reception area
   - A printed receipt will be issued for every cash transaction

2. DEBIT CARD
   - We accept debit cards issued by Nigerian banks (Visa,
     Mastercard, Verve)
   - Card payments are processed via our POS terminals at
     the cashier's desk and pharmacy counter
   - Please ensure your card is enabled for transactions
     before coming in

3. BANK TRANSFER
   - Patients may make payments via direct bank transfer to
     the hospital's designated account
   - Bank transfer details are available at the cashier's desk
     and on request via email: support@medcarespecialist.com
   - Proof of transfer (screenshot or bank teller slip) must be
     presented to the cashier to confirm payment
   - Transfers are typically confirmed within a few minutes
     to a few hours depending on the bank

4. POS (Point of Sale) TERMINAL
   - POS terminals are available at:
     * Main cashier's desk (Ground Floor)
     * Pharmacy counter
     * Emergency billing desk
   - POS transactions require a valid PIN or contactless payment

5. HEALTH INSURANCE / HMO
   - Patients covered under accepted Health Maintenance Organization
     (HMO) or insurance plans may use their coverage to pay for
     eligible services
   - Please present your valid insurance or HMO card at
     registration before receiving any service
   - Pre-authorization may be required for certain procedures
   - Any co-payments or services not covered by your plan must
     be paid out of pocket using any of the above methods
   - See the Insurance Policy document for full details on
     accepted providers and coverage

NOTE:
- Cryptocurrency and foreign currency payments are not accepted
- Personal cheques are not accepted
- All payments must be settled in full before results, prescriptions,
  or discharge documents are released, unless prior arrangements
  have been made


=========================================================
OUTPATIENT BILLING PROCESS
=========================================================
For outpatient visits, billing follows this general process:

Step 1: Registration
  - Patient registers at the front desk and provides personal
    and insurance details (if applicable)
  - A patient file is created or retrieved

Step 2: Consultation Fee Payment
  - Consultation fee is paid at the cashier before seeing the doctor
  - Fee varies by department and specialist level
  - HMO/insured patients proceed after authorization confirmation

Step 3: Service Delivery
  - Patient sees the doctor, undergoes tests, scans, or procedures
    as prescribed

Step 4: Additional Billing
  - If additional services (lab tests, radiology, pharmacy) are
    prescribed, separate payments are made at the respective
    service counters
  - Itemized receipts are issued for each service

Step 5: Receipt and Exit
  - Patient collects all receipts and relevant documents
  - Electronic receipts can be sent via SMS or email upon request


=========================================================
INPATIENT BILLING PROCESS
=========================================================
For patients admitted to the hospital, billing is handled as follows:

ADMISSION DEPOSIT:
  - An initial deposit is required at the time of admission
  - The deposit amount is estimated based on the expected duration
    of stay, ward type, and anticipated procedures
  - Deposit amounts vary — please inquire at the admissions desk
    for current estimates

DURING ADMISSION:
  - Charges accumulate daily for:
    * Ward/bed fees (varies by ward type: general, semi-private,
      private, ICU)
    * Nursing care
    * Medications dispensed from pharmacy
    * Diagnostic tests and imaging
    * Surgical procedures (if applicable)
    * Specialist consultations
    * Consumables and medical supplies
  - Patients or next of kin may request an interim bill at any
    point during admission

TOP-UP PAYMENTS:
  - If the accumulated charges exceed the initial deposit, the
    patient or next of kin will be notified to make a top-up
    payment promptly
  - Delays in top-up payments may affect the continuation of
    certain elective services

DISCHARGE BILLING:
  - A final comprehensive bill is generated upon discharge
  - The patient or next of kin reviews and confirms the bill
  - All outstanding balances must be cleared before discharge
    documents, prescriptions, and medical reports are released
  - Payment can be made via any accepted payment method

INVOICES AND RECEIPTS
=========================================================
MedCare Specialist Hospital provides clear and itemized financial
documentation for all transactions.

INVOICES:
  - Patients may request a formal invoice for any service received
  - Invoices are itemized and include:
    * Patient name and ID
    * Date(s) of service
    * Description of each service rendered
    * Unit cost and total cost
    * Payment status
  - Invoices are useful for insurance reimbursement claims,
    corporate billing, and personal financial records

HOW TO REQUEST AN INVOICE:
  - Visit the billing/cashier's desk at the hospital
  - Call +234 800 MEDCARE and request an invoice by phone
  - Email support@medcarespecialist.com with:
    Subject: "Invoice Request – [Your Full Name] – [Date of Service]"

ELECTRONIC RECEIPTS:
  - Electronic receipts are available for all transactions
  - Receipts can be sent via:
    * SMS to your registered phone number
    * Email to your registered email address
  - To receive an electronic receipt, inform the cashier at the
    time of payment or request one afterward via email or phone

Corporate Account Billing:

REFUND POLICY
=========================================================
MedCare Specialist Hospital understands that circumstances change
and we are committed to processing refunds fairly and promptly
where applicable.

ELIGIBLE REFUND SITUATIONS:
  - Overpayment by the patient
  - Cancellation of a service or procedure that was paid for
    in advance (e.g., booked surgery that was cancelled by
    the hospital)
  - Duplicate payments
  - Insurance settlement that covers a previously self-paid service

NON-REFUNDABLE SITUATIONS:
  - Consultation fees once the consultation has taken place
  - Laboratory or radiology fees once the test/scan has been
    processed
  - Medications dispensed from the pharmacy (for safety and
    hygiene reasons)
  - Late cancellation fees (where applicable)

REFUND PROCESS:
  Step 1: Submit a refund request at the billing/cashier's desk
          or via email at support@medcarespecialist.com
  Step 2: Provide proof of payment (receipt or bank transfer
          confirmation)
  Step 3: State the reason for the refund request clearly
  Step 4: The billing department reviews the request
  Step 5: Approved refunds are processed within seven (7)
          working days

REFUND METHODS:
  - Refunds are issued via the same method used for the original
    payment where possible
  - Cash refunds are available for cash payments
  - Bank transfers are used for card or transfer payments
  - Refund to card may take additional time depending on
    the patient's bank processing timeline

REFUND ENQUIRIES:
  - Phone:  +234 800 MEDCARE
  - Email:  support@medcarespecialist.com
  - Please quote your patient ID and original receipt number
    when making a refund enquiry

=========================================================
BILLING DISPUTES
=========================================================
If you believe there is an error in your bill, please follow
these steps:

Step 1: Review your itemized invoice or receipt carefully
Step 2: Contact the billing desk in person or by phone/email
Step 3: Provide your patient ID, invoice number, and details
        of the disputed charge
Step 4: The billing team will review and respond within
        three (3) working days
Step 5: If the dispute is resolved in your favor, a corrected
        invoice will be issued and any overpayment refunded
        within seven (7) working days

Billing Desk Contact:
  Phone:  +234 800 MEDCARE
  Email:  support@medcarespecialist.com


=========================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED BILLING QUESTIONS
=========================================================

Q: Can I pay my bill in installments?
A: Installment payments are not standard policy but may be
   considered for inpatient cases on a case-by-case basis.
   Please speak with the billing manager.

Q: Can I get a receipt if I paid through bank transfer?
A: Yes. Present your proof of transfer at the cashier's desk
   and an official receipt will be issued.

Q: What happens if I cannot pay at discharge?
A: Please inform the billing team immediately. We will work
   with you to find a suitable arrangement. However, discharge
   documents may be withheld until payment is resolved.

Q: Do you charge extra for using a debit card or POS?
A: No additional charges apply for using a debit card or POS
   terminal at MedCare Specialist Hospital.

Q: Can my employer pay my bill directly?
A: Yes, if your employer has an active corporate account or
   health agreement with the hospital. Please inform the
   billing desk at registration.

Q: How long does a bank transfer refund take?
A: Refunds are processed within seven (7) working days.
   Additional time may be required depending on your bank.

Q: Will I receive a bill by email?
A: Yes. Electronic invoices and receipts can be sent to your
   registered email address upon request.
