ABOUT THE HOSPITAL
------------------
MedCare Specialist Hospital is a leading private multi-specialty hospital
located in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. Founded in 2014, the hospital
was established with a mission to provide high-quality, accessible, and
compassionate healthcare to individuals, families, and corporate clients
across Rivers State and the wider Niger Delta region.

With a capacity of 180 beds and a team of experienced medical professionals,
MedCare Specialist Hospital offers a broad range of medical services spanning
general medicine, specialist consultations, surgery, diagnostics, maternal
care, and emergency medicine. The hospital is equipped with modern medical
technology and staffed by dedicated doctors, nurses, and support personnel
committed to patient safety and comfort.
