MEDCARE SPECIALIST HOSPITAL — PHARMACY SERVICES
================================================

The Pharmacy Department at MedCare Specialist Hospital is a fully
licensed and professionally managed pharmaceutical facility. Staffed
by qualified and registered pharmacists and pharmacy technicians, our
pharmacy is dedicated to providing safe, effective, and affordable
medications alongside excellent pharmaceutical care to all patients.

We serve both inpatients and outpatients and are open seven days a
week for your convenience.

SERVICES OFFERED BY THE PHARMACY
=========================================================

---------------------------------------------------------
SERVICE 1: PRESCRIPTION MEDICATION DISPENSING
---------------------------------------------------------
Our pharmacy dispenses a wide range of prescription medications
covering all major therapeutic categories.

How it works:
  Step 1: Obtain a valid prescription from your doctor at
          MedCare Specialist Hospital or from an external
          licensed physician
  Step 2: Present the prescription at the pharmacy counter
  Step 3: The pharmacist reviews the prescription for
          accuracy, dosage, and potential drug interactions
  Step 4: Pay for the medication at the pharmacy counter
          or cashier's desk
  Step 5: Collect your medication along with clear dispensing
          instructions from the pharmacist

Accepted Prescriptions:
  - Prescriptions issued by MedCare Specialist Hospital doctors
  - Valid prescriptions from licensed external physicians
  - Electronic prescriptions (where applicable)
  - HMO/insurance prescriptions (subject to plan coverage)

Important Notes:
  - Prescriptions for controlled substances require additional
    verification and documentation
  - Prescriptions are valid for a specified period — expired
    prescriptions will not be honored
  - Alterations or tampering with prescriptions is illegal
    and will be reported to the appropriate authorities
  - The pharmacist reserves the right to verify a prescription
    with the issuing doctor before dispensing


---------------------------------------------------------
SERVICE 2: OVER-THE-COUNTER (OTC) MEDICATIONS
---------------------------------------------------------
Patients and visitors may purchase a wide range of over-the-counter
medications without a prescription.

Commonly available OTC medications include:
  - Analgesics and antipyretics (paracetamol, ibuprofen, aspirin)
  - Antacids and indigestion remedies
  - Antihistamines (allergy relief)
  - Cough and cold preparations
  - Oral rehydration salts (ORS)
  - Multivitamins and mineral supplements
  - Antimalarials (for prophylaxis — subject to pharmacist advice)
  - Topical creams and ointments
  - Antiseptics and wound care products
  - Eye and ear drops (selected OTC preparations)
  - Laxatives and antidiarrheal medications
  - Medicated shampoos and skin care products

Important Notes:
  - Our pharmacists are available to advise on the appropriate
    OTC medication for your symptoms
  - Self-medication is acceptable for minor ailments but patients
    with persistent or severe symptoms are encouraged to see a doctor
  - OTC medications are sold at the pharmacy counter without
    a prescription but pharmacist guidance is always available


---------------------------------------------------------
SERVICE 3: VACCINES AND IMMUNIZATION SUPPLIES
---------------------------------------------------------
The Pharmacy Department stocks and supplies vaccines in
coordination with the clinical departments.

Available vaccines and immunization products include:
  - Routine childhood vaccines (in coordination with Pediatrics)
  - Hepatitis B vaccine
  - Tetanus toxoid
  - Yellow fever vaccine (referral or direct supply)
  - Typhoid vaccine
  - Influenza (flu) vaccine (seasonal availability)
  - COVID-19 booster doses (subject to availability)
  - Travel vaccines (on request and subject to availability)
  - Rabies post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) — on prescription

Note:
  - Vaccine administration is carried out by trained nursing
    and medical staff in the appropriate clinical area
  - The pharmacy provides the vaccine supply while clinical
    staff administer injections
  - Cold chain integrity is strictly maintained for all
    temperature-sensitive vaccines
  - Please consult your doctor or the pharmacist for advice
    on which vaccines are appropriate for you or your child


---------------------------------------------------------
SERVICE 4: MEDICATION COUNSELING
---------------------------------------------------------
Our registered pharmacists provide comprehensive medication
counseling to all patients to ensure safe and effective use
of medications.

Counseling includes:
  - How to take your medication correctly (dose, timing,
    route of administration)
  - What to do if you miss a dose
  - Possible side effects and what to watch out for
  - Drug-food interactions (e.g., medications to take with
    or without food)
  - Drug-drug interactions (especially for patients on
    multiple medications)
  - Storage instructions for medications
  - Duration of treatment and importance of completing
    the full course
  - What to do in case of an adverse reaction

Who should request counseling:
  - Patients starting a new medication
  - Patients managing chronic conditions (diabetes,
    hypertension, asthma, HIV, etc.)
  - Patients on multiple medications (polypharmacy)
  - Elderly patients or caregivers of young children
  - Patients who have questions or concerns about
    their medications

Counseling is provided free of charge at the point
of dispensing and by appointment for more detailed sessions.


---------------------------------------------------------
SERVICE 5: PRESCRIPTION REFILL SERVICE
---------------------------------------------------------
Patients who require ongoing medication for chronic conditions
can take advantage of our convenient prescription refill service.

How the refill service works:
  Step 1: Present your original prescription or previous
          pharmacy receipt at the pharmacy counter
  Step 2: The pharmacist reviews your refill eligibility
          (based on the prescribing doctor's instructions)
  Step 3: If a refill is authorized, your medication is
          dispensed
  Step 4: If a new or updated prescription is required,
          you will be directed to book a consultation with
          your doctor before the refill can be processed

Refill Policy:
  - Refills are only processed for medications prescribed
    by a licensed physician
  - Controlled substances cannot be refilled without a
    new prescription from the doctor each time
  - Patients on long-term medications are advised to
    book a follow-up appointment with their doctor at
    regular intervals to review their treatment
  - Refill requests can also be initiated by calling
    +234 800 MEDCARE in advance to confirm availability

Conditions commonly managed with refill services:
  - Hypertension (antihypertensive medications)
  - Diabetes (insulin, metformin, and other antidiabetics)
  - Asthma (inhalers and bronchodilators)
  - Epilepsy (anticonvulsants)
  - HIV/AIDS (antiretroviral therapy — ART)
  - Thyroid disorders (levothyroxine and related medications)
  - Mental health conditions (antidepressants, anxiolytics)
  - Heart conditions (statins, beta-blockers, etc.)


---------------------------------------------------------
SERVICE 6: DRUG INTERACTION AND SAFETY REVIEW
---------------------------------------------------------
Our pharmacists conduct thorough drug interaction and safety
checks for all dispensed medications.

What this includes:
  - Checking for interactions between prescribed medications
  - Identifying contraindications based on the patient's
    medical history (where available)
  - Flagging potential allergic reactions
  - Verifying appropriate dosage for the patient's age,
    weight, and kidney/liver function
  - Recommending dose adjustments or alternatives where
    necessary (in consultation with the prescribing doctor)

Patients are encouraged to:
  - Inform the pharmacist of ALL medications they are
    currently taking (prescription and OTC)
  - Disclose any known drug allergies
  - Mention any herbal remedies, supplements, or
    traditional medicines being used
  - Inform the pharmacist if they are pregnant or
    breastfeeding


---------------------------------------------------------
SERVICE 7: INPATIENT PHARMACY SERVICES
---------------------------------------------------------
For patients admitted to the hospital, the pharmacy provides
dedicated inpatient medication services.

How inpatient pharmacy works:
  - Medications prescribed during admission are dispensed
    directly to the ward nursing team
  - Nursing staff administer medications to patients as
    per the doctor's medication chart
  - Medication charges are tracked and added to the
    patient's inpatient bill
  - Discharge medications are dispensed by the pharmacy
    and handed to the patient with full counseling

Discharge Medication Process:
  Step 1: Attending doctor prepares a discharge prescription
  Step 2: The prescription is sent to the pharmacy
  Step 3: Pharmacy prepares and dispenses the medications
  Step 4: Pharmacist counsels the patient on how to take
          each medication at home
  Step 5: Patient collects medications and proceeds
          to complete the discharge process


---------------------------------------------------------
SERVICE 8: CHRONIC DISEASE MEDICATION MANAGEMENT
---------------------------------------------------------
MedCare Specialist Hospital Pharmacy supports patients with
long-term medical conditions through structured medication
management services.

Services include:
  - Regular medication reviews
  - Adherence counseling (helping patients stick to their
    medication schedule)
  - Monitoring for side effects or treatment complications
  - Coordination with the patient's doctor for dose
    adjustments
  - Education on lifestyle modifications that complement
    drug therapy

Conditions supported:
  - Hypertension and cardiovascular disease
  - Type 1 and Type 2 Diabetes
  - Asthma and COPD
  - HIV/AIDS (antiretroviral therapy)
  - Tuberculosis (TB) treatment
  - Mental health conditions
  - Chronic kidney disease
  - Thyroid disorders
  - Epilepsy and seizure disorders

MEDICATION STORAGE AND SAFETY TIPS
=========================================================
Our pharmacists advise all patients on proper medication
storage and safety practices:

GENERAL STORAGE GUIDELINES:
  - Store medications in a cool, dry place away from
    direct sunlight and moisture
  - Keep medications out of reach of children and pets
  - Do not store medications in the bathroom or kitchen
    where heat and humidity can affect them
  - Refrigerate medications that require cold storage
    (your pharmacist will advise you)
  - Never transfer medications out of their original
    labeled packaging

MEDICATION SAFETY:
  - Never share prescription medications with others
  - Do not take medications prescribed for someone else
  - Complete the full course of antibiotics even if you
    feel better before finishing
  - Do not crush, chew, or split tablets unless advised
    by the pharmacist
  - Check expiry dates before taking any medication

DISPOSAL OF MEDICATIONS:
  - Do not flush medications down the toilet or drain
  - Do not throw medications in the household waste
  - Return unused or expired medications to the pharmacy
    for safe disposal
  - The pharmacy accepts returned medications for
    proper pharmaceutical waste disposal


=========================================================
DRUG ALLERGY INFORMATION
=========================================================
Patient safety is our highest priority. All patients are
encouraged to:

  - Inform the pharmacist and doctor of any known drug
    allergies BEFORE receiving any medication
  - Describe the nature of the allergic reaction
    (e.g., rash, swelling, difficulty breathing)
  - Carry a list of medications they are allergic to
    at all times
  - Wear a medical alert bracelet if they have severe
    drug allergies

If you experience an allergic reaction after taking
a medication dispensed from our pharmacy:

  - Stop taking the medication immediately
  - Proceed to the Emergency Department or call
    112 / +234 800 MEDCARE immediately
  - Inform the emergency team of the medication taken
    and the nature of the reaction
  - Report the reaction to the pharmacy so that it can
    be documented in your medical record

FREQUENTLY ASKED PHARMACY QUESTIONS
=========================================================

Q: Can I get medication without a prescription?
A: Yes, for over-the-counter (OTC) medications. Prescription
   medications require a valid prescription from a licensed
   doctor. Our pharmacist can advise you on the best OTC
   option for minor symptoms.

Q: What if the medication I need is out of stock?
A: Our pharmacist will advise you on an equivalent alternative
   or provide an estimated restock date. We can also assist
   with referrals to external pharmacies if needed.

Q: Can I return medications I no longer need?
A: Medications that have been dispensed generally cannot
   be returned for safety and hygiene reasons. However,
   you may return them for safe disposal. Refunds for
   returned medications are not typically issued.

Q: Does the pharmacy honor prescriptions from other hospitals?
A: Yes, we accept valid prescriptions from licensed external
   physicians. The pharmacist may verify the prescription
   with the issuing doctor if needed.

Q: How do I know if my insurance covers my medication?
A: Present your HMO card at the pharmacy counter. The
   pharmacist will verify coverage with your insurer.
   You can also contact your insurer directly to confirm
   which medications are on their approved drug list.

Q: Can I request a medication refill over the phone?
A: You can call +234 800 MEDCARE to check medication
   availability and initiate a refill request. However,
   you will need to visit the pharmacy in person to
   collect your medication and complete payment.

Q: What should I do if I accidentally take too much
   of my medication?
A: Go to the Emergency Department immediately or call
   112 / +234 800 MEDCARE. Bring your medication
   container with you so the medical team can identify
   what was taken and administer appropriate treatment.

Q: Are generic medications available?
A: Yes. The pharmacy stocks both branded and generic
   medications. Generic medications contain the same
   active ingredient as branded versions and are equally
   effective. Ask the pharmacist about generic options
   to reduce costs.

Q: Can the pharmacy deliver medications to my ward?
A: Yes. For admitted inpatients, medications are
   delivered directly to the ward nursing team.
   Outpatient home delivery is not currently available
   but may be arranged in special circumstances —
   please speak with the pharmacy team.

Q: Is the pharmacy open on Sundays and public holidays?
A: Yes. The pharmacy operates every day of the week
   including Sundays and public holidays from
   8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
