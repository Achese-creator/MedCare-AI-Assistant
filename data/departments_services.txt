MEDCARE SPECIALIST HOSPITAL — DEPARTMENTS & SERVICES
=====================================================

MedCare Specialist Hospital operates twelve (12) specialized departments,
each staffed with qualified professionals and equipped with modern facilities.
Below is a comprehensive guide to each department and the services offered.


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 1: GENERAL MEDICINE
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, Ground Floor
Head:         Department of Internal Medicine
Hours:        Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM
              Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

The Department of General Medicine serves as the primary point of contact
for patients seeking medical attention. Our internists manage a wide range
of common illnesses, chronic conditions, and preventive care.

Services Provided:
- General consultations (walk-in and scheduled)
- Chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension, asthma, etc.)
- Fever and infection treatment
- Gastrointestinal disorders
- Respiratory conditions
- Thyroid disorders
- Preventive health counseling
- Referrals to specialist departments
- Executive and routine medical checkups
- Corporate health packages


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 2: CARDIOLOGY
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, First Floor
Head:         Consultant Cardiologist
Hours:        Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
              Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM (by appointment)

The Cardiology Department specializes in the diagnosis, treatment, and
prevention of diseases affecting the heart and blood vessels.

Services Provided:
- Specialist cardiology consultations
- Electrocardiogram (ECG)
- Echocardiography (cardiac ultrasound)
- Cardiac biomarker testing
- Blood pressure monitoring and management
- Heart failure management
- Arrhythmia evaluation
- Pre-operative cardiac assessment
- Stress testing (by referral)
- Post-cardiac event rehabilitation
- Referral for advanced cardiac procedures (e.g., angiography, stenting)


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 3: PEDIATRICS
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, Ground Floor
Head:         Consultant Pediatrician
Hours:        Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM
              Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

The Pediatrics Department provides comprehensive healthcare for infants,
children, and adolescents from birth up to 18 years of age.

Services Provided:
- Pediatric consultations
- Growth and development monitoring
- Childhood immunization and vaccination schedules
- Treatment of common childhood illnesses (malaria, respiratory infections,
  diarrhea, etc.)
- Nutritional guidance for children
- Allergy management
- Asthma management in children
- Neonatal care follow-up
- School health screening
- Referrals for pediatric surgery or specialty care


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 4: OBSTETRICS & GYNECOLOGY (OB/GYN)
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, First Floor
Head:         Consultant Obstetrician / Gynecologist
Hours:        Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
              Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM (by appointment)

The Obstetrics & Gynecology Department provides comprehensive care for
women's health, including pregnancy, childbirth, and reproductive health.

Services Provided:
- Antenatal care (ANC) consultations
- Postnatal care
- Normal delivery services
- Caesarean section (C-section)
- Family planning counseling and services
- Fertility assessment (basic)
- Pap smear / cervical screening
- Breast examination and screening
- Management of menstrual disorders
- Treatment of gynecological infections
- Menopause management
- Ultrasound scans during pregnancy
- High-risk pregnancy management
- Referral for advanced obstetric procedures


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 5: ORTHOPEDICS
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, Ground Floor
Head:         Consultant Orthopedic Surgeon
Hours:        Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
              Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM (by appointment)

The Orthopedics Department specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of
musculoskeletal conditions including bones, joints, muscles, ligaments,
and tendons.

Services Provided:
- Orthopedic consultations
- Fracture management (casting, splinting, surgical fixation)
- Joint pain and arthritis management
- Back and spine care
- Sports injury treatment
- Dislocation reduction
- Orthopedic surgery (minor and major)
- Pre- and post-surgical orthopedic care
- Referral for physiotherapy
- Referral for advanced orthopedic procedures (e.g., joint replacement)


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 6: SURGERY
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, First Floor (Theatre Suite)
Head:         Consultant General Surgeon
Hours:        Scheduled surgeries: Monday–Friday
              Emergency surgery: 24/7

The Surgery Department provides a full range of surgical services, from
minor outpatient procedures to major inpatient operations. Our surgical
team works in state-of-the-art operating theatres equipped with modern
anesthesia and monitoring equipment.

Services Provided:
- General surgical consultations
- Minor surgery (e.g., wound suturing, abscess drainage, cyst removal,
  biopsy)
- Major surgery (e.g., appendectomy, hernia repair, laparotomy)
- Caesarean section (in collaboration with OB/GYN)
- Emergency surgical intervention (trauma, appendicitis, bowel obstruction,
  etc.)
- Pre-operative assessment and planning
- Post-operative care and follow-up
- Referral for specialized surgical sub-specialties (neuro, cardiothoracic,
  urological) when required


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 7: LABORATORY
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, Ground Floor
Head:         Chief Medical Laboratory Scientist
Hours:        Daily: 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM

The Laboratory Department is a fully equipped diagnostic facility that
supports all hospital departments with accurate and timely test results.

Services Provided:
- Full Blood Count (FBC)
- Lipid Profile
- Blood Sugar (Fasting & Random)
- Malaria Test (RDT and Microscopy)
- Urinalysis
- Liver Function Test (LFT)
- Kidney Function Test (KFT)
- ECG (Electrocardiogram)
- Cardiac Biomarkers (Troponin, CK-MB)
- Hepatitis B and C screening
- HIV screening
- Pregnancy test (urine and blood)
- Stool microscopy
- Electrolytes, Urea, and Creatinine (E/U/Cr)
- HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)
- Culture and Sensitivity testing
- Blood grouping and cross-matching
- Prothrombin Time (PT/INR)
- Thyroid function tests (TSH, T3, T4)
- Corporate and pre-employment medical screening
- Specimen collection for advanced/external testing


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 8: PHARMACY
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, Ground Floor (near main entrance)
Head:         Chief Pharmacist
Hours:        Daily: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM

The Pharmacy Department provides prescription dispensing, over-the-counter
medications, and pharmaceutical counseling to both inpatients and outpatients.

Services Provided:
- Prescription medication dispensing
- Over-the-counter (OTC) medicine sales
- Vaccines and immunization supplies
- Medication counseling and education
- Prescription refill service
- Drug interaction checks
- Medication safety reviews
- Chronic disease medication management (e.g., antihypertensives,
  antidiabetics, antimalarials)
- Dispensing of antiretroviral medications
- Availability of essential and emergency medications


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 9: RADIOLOGY
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, Ground Floor
Head:         Consultant Radiologist
Hours:        Daily: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM

The Radiology Department offers diagnostic imaging services to support
accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

Services Provided:
- X-ray (plain radiography)
- Ultrasound scanning
- CT Scan
- MRI referrals (in partnership with external imaging centers)
- Echocardiography (in coordination with Cardiology)
- Mammography (referral)
- Fluoroscopy (when available)
- Image interpretation and reporting
- Emergency imaging services
- Corporate and pre-employment imaging screening


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 10: PHYSIOTHERAPY
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, Ground Floor
Head:         Chief Physiotherapist
Hours:        Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
              Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM

The Physiotherapy Department provides rehabilitation and physical therapy
services to help patients recover from injuries, surgeries, and chronic
musculoskeletal conditions.

Services Provided:
- Musculoskeletal physiotherapy (back pain, joint pain, neck pain)
- Post-surgical rehabilitation
- Sports injury rehabilitation
- Stroke rehabilitation
- Neurological physiotherapy
- Pediatric physiotherapy
- Geriatric physiotherapy
- Pain management therapy
- Exercise prescription and patient education
- Home exercise program development
- Referral coordination with Orthopedics, Neurology, and Surgery


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 11: DENTAL CLINIC
=========================================================
Location:     Main Building, Ground Floor
Head:         Consulting Dental Surgeon
Hours:        Monday–Friday: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
              Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM (by appointment)

The Dental Clinic provides general and preventive dental care for patients
of all ages.

Services Provided:
- Dental consultations
- Dental examination and diagnosis
- Teeth cleaning and polishing (scaling and prophylaxis)
- Tooth extraction
- Dental fillings (restoration)
- Root canal treatment
- Dental X-rays
- Gum disease treatment
- Pediatric dental care
- Oral health education
- Referral for advanced dental procedures (orthodontics, oral surgery)


=========================================================
DEPARTMENT 12: EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT
=========================================================
Location:     Emergency Wing (Ground Floor, separate entrance)
Head:         Emergency Medicine Physician
Hours:        24 hours, 7 days a week

The Emergency Department is the frontline of MedCare Specialist Hospital,
providing immediate medical attention for life-threatening and urgent
conditions around the clock.

Services Provided:
- Emergency medical assessment and triage
- Cardiac emergency response
- Stroke emergency response
- Trauma care
- Emergency surgical intervention
- Ambulance services
- Emergency resuscitation and stabilization
- Wound care and suturing
- Fracture and dislocation emergency management
- Poisoning and overdose management
- Obstetric emergencies
- Pediatric emergencies
- Transfer and referral coordination
- Emergency ambulance dispatch: Call 112 or +234 800 MEDCARE
