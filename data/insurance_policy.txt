MEDCARE SPECIALIST HOSPITAL — INSURANCE POLICY
===============================================

MedCare Specialist Hospital works with a number of Health Maintenance
Organizations (HMOs) and insurance providers to ensure that quality
healthcare is accessible and affordable to insured patients. This
document outlines the insurance providers we accept, how to use your
insurance at our facility, the claims process, and important information
about coverage limitations.

We encourage all patients to familiarize themselves with their individual
insurance plans and to contact their insurer directly for specific
coverage details.


=========================================================
ACCEPTED INSURANCE PROVIDERS
=========================================================
MedCare Specialist Hospital currently accepts the following Health
Maintenance Organizations (HMOs) and insurance providers:

1. NHIA (National Health Insurance Authority)
   - Formerly known as NHIS (National Health Insurance Scheme)
   - Covers eligible enrollees under the national health insurance
     framework
   - Primary care and specialist referrals available under this scheme
   - Enrollees must present a valid NHIA card and enrollment number

2. AXA MANSARD HEALTH
   - One of Nigeria's leading health insurance providers
   - Wide range of individual, family, and corporate health plans
   - Covers consultations, diagnostics, medications, and inpatient care
     (subject to plan limits)
   - Pre-authorization may be required for specialist consultations
     and surgical procedures

3. HYGEIA HMO
   - A trusted HMO with extensive network coverage across Nigeria
   - Covers outpatient consultations, laboratory services, pharmacy,
     and inpatient care (subject to plan type)
   - Referral letters may be required for specialist services
   - Enrollees must present valid Hygeia HMO ID card

4. AVON HMO
   - Provides comprehensive health coverage for individuals and
     corporate groups
   - Covers general consultations, diagnostics, medications, and
     hospital admissions (subject to benefit limits)
   - Pre-authorization required for elective procedures and surgeries
   - Enrollees must present valid Avon HMO card at registration

5. RELIANCE HMO
   - A digital-first HMO offering flexible health plans
   - Covers primary care, specialist consultations, laboratory tests,
     and pharmacy services (subject to plan)
   - Enrollees may need to verify coverage via the Reliance HMO app
     or customer service before visiting
   - Digital or physical HMO card accepted

6. LEADWAY HEALTH
   - Part of the Leadway Assurance Group
   - Offers individual and group health plans with broad coverage
   - Covers consultations, diagnostics, medications, surgeries, and
     inpatient care (subject to plan limits)
   - Pre-authorization required for major procedures
   - Enrollees must present valid Leadway Health ID card


=========================================================
IMPORTANT NOTE ON COVERAGE
=========================================================
- The services covered under each insurance plan vary depending on
  the specific plan type (e.g., basic, standard, premium, executive)
- MedCare Specialist Hospital is not responsible for determining
  what your individual plan covers
- Patients are strongly advised to contact their insurance provider
  or HMO BEFORE visiting to confirm:
    * Whether MedCare Specialist Hospital is in their provider network
    * Which services are covered under their specific plan
    * Whether pre-authorization or a referral letter is required
    * What co-payment amounts apply to their plan

  out of pocket using accepted payment methods


=========================================================
HOW TO USE YOUR INSURANCE AT MEDCARE
=========================================================
Using your health insurance or HMO at MedCare Specialist Hospital
is straightforward. Follow the steps below:

STEP 1: VERIFY YOUR COVERAGE BEFORE YOUR VISIT
  - Contact your HMO or insurance provider to confirm that
    MedCare Specialist Hospital is an accredited facility under
    your plan
  - Confirm which services are covered and whether you need a
    referral or pre-authorization letter

STEP 2: BRING THE REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
  When you arrive at the hospital, please bring the following:
  - Valid HMO or insurance ID card (physical or digital)
  - Your enrollee ID number
  - Any referral or authorization letter from your HMO (if required)
  - A valid government-issued ID
  - Previous medical records or test results (if applicable)

STEP 3: REGISTER AT THE FRONT DESK
  - Inform the receptionist that you are an insured patient
  - Present your HMO card and any required authorization documents
  - The receptionist will verify your enrollment status with
    your insurance provider
  - If your coverage is confirmed, you will be directed to the
    appropriate department

STEP 4: RECEIVE CARE
  - Proceed to the relevant department for consultation,
    diagnostics, or treatment as directed
  - Only services covered under your plan will be billed to
    your insurance provider
  - Any additional services outside your coverage will be
    communicated to you before they are rendered

STEP 5: CO-PAYMENT (IF APPLICABLE)
  - Some insurance plans require a co-payment at the point
    of service
  - Co-payment amounts vary by plan and service type
  - Co-payments must be paid using any of our accepted payment
    methods (cash, debit card, POS, or bank transfer)

STEP 6: DISCHARGE AND DOCUMENTATION
  - Upon completion of your visit or discharge, the hospital
    will submit the necessary claims documentation to your
    insurance provider
  - You will receive a copy of your treatment summary and
    any relevant documents for your personal records


=========================================================
PRE-AUTHORIZATION REQUIREMENTS
=========================================================
Certain medical services require pre-authorization from your
insurance provider or HMO before they can be covered. These
typically include:

- Elective surgical procedures (major and minor)
- Specialist consultations (depending on your HMO plan)
- Inpatient admission (planned admissions)
- Advanced diagnostic procedures (CT scan, MRI referral,
  Echocardiography)
- Physiotherapy sessions (extended courses)
- Dental procedures beyond basic consultation

HOW TO OBTAIN PRE-AUTHORIZATION:
  1. Your attending doctor at MedCare will recommend the
     required procedure or referral
  2. Contact your insurance provider or HMO directly to
     request pre-authorization
  3. Provide the insurer with the details of the recommended
     service and the hospital's information
  4. Once authorization is granted, bring the authorization
     letter or code to the hospital
  5. Present the authorization to the billing desk before
     the procedure begins

NOTE:
  - Pre-authorization does not guarantee full payment by
    the insurer — final settlement is subject to your
    plan's terms and conditions
  - Emergency procedures do not require pre-authorization.
    The hospital will notify your insurer as soon as
    practicable in emergency situations


=========================================================
INSURANCE CLAIMS PROCESS
=========================================================
MedCare Specialist Hospital assists patients with the insurance
claims process to the extent possible. Here is how claims
are typically handled:

DIRECT BILLING CLAIMS (HMO Network Patients):
  - For patients whose insurers have a direct billing agreement
    with MedCare Specialist Hospital, claims are submitted
    directly by the hospital to the insurance provider
  - The patient is only responsible for any applicable
    co-payments or non-covered services
  - No additional paperwork is required from the patient
    for direct billing claims

REIMBURSEMENT CLAIMS (Out-of-Network or Self-Pay Patients):
  - If you paid out of pocket and wish to seek reimbursement
    from your insurer, you will need to submit a claim directly
    to your insurance provider
  - MedCare Specialist Hospital will provide all necessary
    documentation to support your claim, including:
      * Itemized invoice/receipt
      * Doctor's report or clinical notes (upon request)
      * Laboratory or radiology reports
      * Discharge summary (for inpatients)
  - To request supporting documents for a reimbursement claim:
      Phone:  +234 800 MEDCARE
      Email:  support@medcarespecialist.com

CLAIMS DOCUMENTATION TURNAROUND:
  - Standard claims documents are prepared within three (3)
    to five (5) working days of request
  - Urgent documentation requests may be escalated — speak
    with the billing manager


=========================================================
CO-PAYMENT INFORMATION
=========================================================
A co-payment is a fixed amount that insured patients are
required to pay out of pocket at the time of service, in
addition to what their insurance covers.

General Co-payment Guidelines:
  - Co-payment amounts are determined by your individual
    insurance plan, not by MedCare Specialist Hospital
  - Co-payments are collected at the cashier's desk before
    or after your consultation
  - Failure to pay the required co-payment may result in
    services being withheld or billed entirely to the patient
  - Please confirm your co-payment obligations with your
    insurer before your visit

Common co-payment examples (illustrative, not fixed):
  - General consultation:        As per your plan
  - Specialist consultation:     As per your plan
  - Laboratory tests:            As per your plan
  - Pharmacy:                    As per your plan

SERVICES TYPICALLY COVERED BY INSURANCE
=========================================================
Coverage varies by plan, but the following services are
commonly covered (subject to your specific plan limits):

USUALLY COVERED:
  - General outpatient consultations
  - Specialist consultations (with referral where required)
  - Basic laboratory tests (FBC, urinalysis, blood sugar,
    malaria test, etc.)
  - Routine radiology (X-ray, ultrasound)
  - Prescription medications (on the insurer's approved
    drug list)
  - Inpatient care and ward charges (up to plan limits)
  - Emergency care (subject to insurer notification)
  - Antenatal care (for applicable plans)
  - Immunization (for applicable plans)

COMMONLY NOT COVERED OR LIMITED:
  - Cosmetic or elective non-medical procedures
  - Dental procedures beyond basic consultation
    (varies by plan)
  - Experimental treatments
  - Non-approved medications
  - Services rendered without required pre-authorization
  - Private room upgrades (may attract additional charges)
  - MRI scans (subject to plan — referrals handled separately)

NOTE:
  Always confirm with your insurer what is and is not
  covered under your specific plan before receiving services.

FREQUENTLY ASKED INSURANCE QUESTIONS
=========================================================

Q: How do I know if MedCare Specialist Hospital accepts my insurance?
A: Check the list of accepted providers above. If your insurer
   is listed, you are covered. You may also call us at
   +234 800 MEDCARE or contact your insurer to confirm.

Q: What if my HMO is not on the accepted list?
A: You may still receive care at MedCare and pay out of pocket.
   You can then seek reimbursement from your insurer directly.
   We will provide all necessary documentation.

Q: Do I need a referral letter to see a specialist?
A: This depends on your insurance plan. Some plans require a
   referral from a primary care provider before a specialist
   consultation will be covered. Please confirm with your HMO.

Q: What happens in an emergency if I don't have my insurance card?
A: Emergency care will be provided immediately regardless of
   insurance status. You can present your insurance details
   afterward and the billing team will assist with claims.

Q: Can my family members use my insurance card?
A: Only enrollees listed on the insurance plan are eligible
   to use coverage. Family plans may cover listed dependents.
   Please confirm with your insurer.

Q: What if my insurance claim is rejected?
A: You will be responsible for settling the outstanding balance.
   The hospital billing team can provide documentation to help
   you appeal the decision with your insurer.

Q: How long does it take for insurance payments to be settled?
A: Settlement timelines vary by insurer. Direct billing claims
   are typically settled within 30 to 60 days. Please follow
   up with your insurer for specific timelines.
